## Color and character references

- [Friendship is Magic Color Guide — MLP Vector Club](https://mlpvector.club/cg/pony/full): coat, mane and accent color references. UI colors are adapted for readable text and controls.
- [Hasbro Friendship Day guide: Twilight Sparkle](https://www.hasbro.com/common/assets/html5/MyLittlePony/friendshipDay_2015/documents/en-us/pg_twilight.pdf): character and friendship-element references.

The small particle silhouettes in the theme are original geometric UI decorations inspired by the characters' motifs. They are not exact copies of the characters' cutie marks.
